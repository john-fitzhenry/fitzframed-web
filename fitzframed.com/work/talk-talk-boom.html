Case Study

TALK TALK BOOM!

Role

Podcast Infrastructure Consultant

Client

FPAC & Raye Lynn Mercer

Tech

Studio Design / Audio Engineering / Multi-cam Capture / Apple Podcasts

The Challenge

FPAC Artistic Director Raye Lynn Mercer wanted to interview Broadway guests and showcase the performing arts world, but lacked podcast infrastructure, a distribution strategy, and in-house technical expertise.

The Solution

Designed the Talk Talk Boom brand and built a portable recording setup at THE BLACK BOX theater. Produced 5 episodes featuring guest artists, published to YouTube and Apple Podcasts, and thoroughly documented the full workflow so FPAC staff can operate entirely independently.

The Result

FPAC gained an owned media channel that deepens audience engagement and extends their reach beyond the theater walls.
